FEE EXPLANATION:
- Free‑tier usage is limited, and limits (including separate limits for image generation, file uploads, data analysis, and voice) can change over time.  
- Plus users have higher usage limits than Free users; if you reach a Free limit, upgrading to Plus, Pro, or Business resets your limits.  
- ChatGPT Plus is a paid subscription, listed in India at ₹1,999 per month (including GST), billed monthly; Plus subscriptions may include usage limits such as message caps, especially during high demand.  
- ChatGPT Go is listed in India at ₹399 per month (inclusive of GST), with more messages, image creation, memory, and storage than the Free tier.  
- You can manage your subscription, view invoices, and cancel from your ChatGPT account; annual billing or paying for multiple months in advance is not supported for Plus.
